R-01: Student account registration and login - Prospective and continuing students can create an account with their university email, verify it, and sign in with a password that meets the agreed complexity policy.
R-02: Course catalogue browsing and search - Students can browse the published course catalogue for a term and search it by course code, course title, subject area and delivery mode.
R-03: Course enrolment and seat allocation - Students can enrol in an open course section, and the system allocates a seat atomically so that the published section capacity is never exceeded.
R-04: Waitlist management - When a section is full the student may join a waitlist, and seats released by a withdrawal are offered to waitlisted students in order of joining.
R-05: Timetable conflict detection - The system rejects an enrolment that would create a timetable clash with another course the student is already enrolled in for the same term.
R-06: Student timetable view and export - Students can view their confirmed weekly timetable for a term and export it as a printable PDF document.
R-07: Prerequisite validation - Enrolment is blocked unless the student has completed, or is currently enrolled in, every prerequisite course recorded against the section.
R-08: Tuition fee calculation - The system calculates the tuition payable for a student's confirmed enrolments for a term using the published fee schedule for their programme.
R-09: Online fee payment by card - Students can pay the calculated tuition balance online by credit or debit card through the university's existing payment gateway.
R-10: Payment receipt generation - A receipt is generated for every successful payment, showing the amount, date, payment reference and the enrolments it covers.
R-11: Administrator course management - Registry administrators can create, edit, publish and withdraw course sections, including capacity, schedule, instructor and prerequisites.
R-12: Faculty class roster view - Teaching faculty can view the confirmed class roster for each section they are assigned to, including student name and programme.
R-13: Email notification on enrolment changes - The system emails the student whenever an enrolment is confirmed, withdrawn, or moved as a result of an administrative change.
R-14: Academic transcript view - Students can view an unofficial transcript listing completed courses, grades awarded and cumulative grade point average.
R-15: Role-based access control - Every screen and service endpoint enforces role-based access control across the student, faculty, administrator and registrar roles.
R-16: Audit logging of enrolment actions - Every enrolment, withdrawal and waitlist movement is written to an append-only audit log recording actor, action, subject and timestamp.
R-17: Accessibility conformance - All student-facing screens conform to WCAG 2.1 level AA, including keyboard operation, visible focus, colour contrast and screen reader labelling.
R-18: Weekly enrolment statistics report - A scheduled job produces a weekly enrolment statistics report for the registrar showing fill rates and waitlist depth by section.
